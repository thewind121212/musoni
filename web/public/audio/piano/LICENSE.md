# Piano samples

These recordings are derived from the **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by Alexander Holm,
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/)
(source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3, Yamaha C5 grand).

Taken from the velocity layer 8 MP3 export published on npm as
`@audio-samples/piano-mp3-velocity8@1.0.5` (packaging by Jan Forst, MIT).

Changes made for Musoni: 16 notes kept (D#2 to C6, one every minor third), leading
silence removed, trimmed to 2 s with a 0.6 s fade-out, downmixed to mono, re-encoded
at 64 kb/s. Files are named with `s` for sharp (`Ds4.mp3` = D#4).
