## This Modified Version

- **Title:** Lý thuyết âm nhạc Musoni / Musoni theory lessons. The app shows the
  lessons as "Lý thuyết âm nhạc" / "Music theory" and never uses the original's
  title for them.
- **Licence:** GNU Free Documentation License, Version 1.3 (as the original allows,
  "or any later version"). No Invariant Sections, no Front-Cover Texts and no
  Back-Cover Texts have been added.
- **Publisher of the modifications:** the Musoni project.
- **What changed:** the text is rewritten as short bilingual lessons (Vietnamese
  first) with practice questions, note names follow the reader's naming
  (solfège or letters), and the musical examples are our own rather than the
  book's.

## Contents adapted

Each lesson file names the book sections it draws on (`sources`), and its end
screen links them.

| Chapter | Folder | Book sections |
|---------|--------|---------------|
| 1. Cao độ & khuông nhạc / Pitch and the staff | `ch01-pitch-staff` | 1.1 Pitch, 1.2 Notation, 1.3 Octave registers; review after 1.6 Practice exercises |
| 2. Dấu hóa, nửa cung, nguyên cung / Accidentals and steps | `ch02-accidentals-steps` | 1.4 Accidentals, 1.5 Enharmonic notes, 2.1 Half steps and whole steps; review after 1.6 and 2.4 Practice exercises |
| 3. Gam trưởng và hóa biểu / Major scales and key signatures | `ch03-major-scales-keys` | 2.2 The major scale, 2.3 Major key signatures (with 2.3.1 Identifying key signatures); review after 2.4 Practice exercises |
| 4. Gam thứ và hóa biểu giọng thứ / Minor scales and key signatures | `ch04-minor-scales-keys` | 3.1 Minor scales, 3.2 Minor key signatures, 3.3 Scale degree names; review after 3.4 Practice exercises |
| 5. Trường độ và nhịp / Basics of rhythm | `ch05-durations-time` | 4.1 Time signature, 4.2 Durational symbols, 4.3 Dots and ties, 4.4 Meter, 4.5 Tuplets, 4.6 Common rhythmic notation errors; review after 4.7 Practice exercises |
| 6. Quãng / Intervals | `ch06-intervals` | 5.1 Introduction to intervals, 5.2 Identifying intervals, 5.3 Writing intervals, 5.4 Inversion of intervals, 5.5 Augmented and diminished intervals; review after 5.6 Practice exercises |
| 7. Hợp âm ba / Triads | `ch07-triads` | 6.1 Introduction to triads, 6.2 Lead-sheet symbols, 6.3 Inverted triads, 6.4 Analyzing chords, 6.5 Simple "sus" chords, 6.6 Summary; review after 6.7 Practice exercises |
| 8. Bậc La Mã và kết / Roman numerals and cadences | `ch08-roman-cadences` | 7.1 Roman numeral chord symbols, 7.2 Diatonic chords in major, 7.3 Diatonic chords in minor, 7.4 Cadences; review after 7.5 Practice exercises |
| 9. Hợp âm bảy / Seventh chords | `ch09-seventh-chords` | 8.1 Introduction to seventh chords, 8.2 The IV/5 "sus" chord, 8.3 Roman numerals of diatonic seventh chords; review after 8.4 Practice exercises |

A chapter PR adds its row here (and the About page lists it on its own, from the
