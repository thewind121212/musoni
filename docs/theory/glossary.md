# Theory glossary (vi ↔ en)

Fixed vocabulary for every theory lesson. Use these words and no synonyms. In
Vietnamese, the first use of a term in a lesson is bold with the English in
brackets: **nửa cung** (half step). English follows the book's American usage.

Note names are never written into lesson text as words. Write a pitch token
(`{G4}`, `{G}`) and the app prints it in the reader's naming (Sol / G).

| gam trưởng | major scale |

Trap: in Vietnamese "giọng song song" means **relative**, not parallel.
